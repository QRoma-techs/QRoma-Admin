Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 May 2026

Qroma Technologies ("we", "our", "us") operates the Qroma restaurant management platform (the "Service"). This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our Service.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information from Cafe Owners (Customers)

When you sign up for a subscription, we collect:

  • Business name and address
  • Owner name, email, and phone number
  • GSTIN and FSSAI numbers (if provided)
  • Payment information (handled by our payment processor — we do not store card details)

1.2 Information from Cafe Customers (End Users)

When end customers use the cafe ordering interface, the cafe may collect:

  • Customer name (entered at table)
  • Phone number (optional, for order tracking)
  • Order history
  • Approximate location (for geofence verification only — not stored)

This data belongs to the cafe, not us. We act as a data processor on behalf of the cafe.

1.3 Technical Data

We automatically collect IP address, browser type, device info, and usage patterns to improve the Service and prevent abuse.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide and maintain the Service
  • To process payments and send invoices
  • To send service updates and security alerts
  • To respond to support requests
  • To improve features and detect fraud

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your information. We share data only with:

  • Service providers: Trusted cloud infrastructure providers (hosting, database, image storage, payment processing) — all under strict data processing agreements
  • Legal compliance: If required by law, court order, or government request
  • Business transfers: In case of merger or acquisition, with prior notice

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on enterprise-grade cloud servers in secured regions. We use HTTPS encryption for all data in transit and industry-standard encryption at rest. While we take reasonable measures, no system is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights

You may:

  • Access your data at any time through your account
  • Request a copy of your data via email
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Export your data in CSV format from the Kitchen Display System
  • Cancel your subscription and request full data deletion (subject to legal retention requirements)

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. Analytics cookies are anonymous and aggregated.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. International Data Transfers

Data may be processed in regions where our cloud service providers operate (primarily India and other secured global data centers). By using the Service, you consent to such transfers.

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be notified via email at least 30 days in advance.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 26 May 2026

This policy explains how subscription cancellations and refunds work for Qroma, operated by Qroma Technologies.

1. Free Trial

All new subscriptions start with a 7-day free trial. No payment is required during the trial. If you cancel before the trial ends, you pay nothing.

2. Subscription Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time:

  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period
  • You retain access to the Service until the end of the period you've already paid for
  • Your data is retained for 30 days after cancellation in case you want to restart
  • After 30 days, data may be permanently deleted

To cancel, email admin@qroma.in from your registered email address.

3. Refund Policy

Subscriptions are non-refundable.

Once a billing period has started, payments are not refunded — neither for full nor partial periods. This is standard for SaaS services and allows us to keep pricing low.

3.1 Exceptions where refunds MAY be issued

  • Duplicate charge: If you were billed twice for the same period due to a technical error, we will refund the duplicate
  • Service unavailability: If the Service is unavailable for more than 7 consecutive days due to our fault, you may request a pro-rated credit
  • Billing error: If you were charged the wrong amount, we will correct and refund the difference

3.2 Refund process

To request a refund under the above exceptions:

  • Email admin@qroma.in within 30 days of the charge
  • Include your registered email, payment reference, and reason
  • We will review within 5 business days
  • If approved, refunds are processed within 7-10 business days to the original payment method

4. Cancellation Due to Non-Payment

If a renewal payment fails:

  • Day 0: Payment fails, we notify you
  • Day 3: Second attempt
  • Day 7: Service suspended
  • Day 30: Account terminated, data may be deleted

You can restore service at any point during this window by completing payment.

5. Downgrades

You can downgrade to a cheaper plan anytime. The change takes effect at the next billing cycle. No refund for the price difference of the current period.

6. Data Export Before Cancellation

You can export all your data (orders, menu, customers) as CSV from the Kitchen Display System at any time. We recommend doing this before cancelling.

7. Disputes

Any disputes regarding charges or refunds shall be resolved per our Terms & Conditions, in the courts of Meerut, India.

8. Contact
